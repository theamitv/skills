# Cloud Savings Strategies Reference

## Quick Wins (Immediate)

| Strategy | Typical Savings | Risk |
|----------|----------------|------|
| Stop idle instances | 10-30% of compute | Low |
| Right-size overprovisioned instances | 10-20% of compute | Low |
| Delete unused storage volumes | 5-10% of storage | Low |
| Remove unused load balancers | 2-5% of networking | Low |
| Clean up old snapshots | 2-5% of storage | Low |

## Medium-Term (30-60 days)

| Strategy | Typical Savings | Risk |
|----------|----------------|------|
| Reserved Instances / Savings Plans | 30-60% of compute | Medium |
| Spot instance adoption | 60-90% of compute | Medium |
| Auto-scaling implementation | 15-30% of compute | Medium |
| Storage tier optimization | 20-40% of storage | Low |
| Database rightsizing | 10-30% of database | Medium |

## Strategic (90+ days)

| Strategy | Typical Savings | Risk |
|----------|----------------|------|
| Kubernetes resource optimization | 20-40% of K8s | Medium |
| Architecture modernization | 20-50% of workload | High |
| Multi-cloud strategy | 10-30% overall | High |
| Observability cost optimization | 20-40% of monitoring | Low |
| AI/ML infrastructure optimization | 20-50% of AI costs | Medium |
