# SaaS Product Templates

## PRD Template Structure

1. Executive Summary
2. Business Context
3. Objectives & Success Metrics
4. User Personas
5. Functional Requirements
6. Non-functional Requirements
7. User Stories
8. Acceptance Criteria
9. Dependencies & Risks
10. Future Scope

## Architecture Decision Record Template

```markdown
# ADR-001: [Title]

## Status
[Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded]

## Context
[What is the issue?]

## Decision
[What was decided?]

## Consequences
[What trade-offs were made?]

## Alternatives Considered
[What else was evaluated?]
```

## Pricing Model Templates

- **Free Tier**: Limited features, usage caps, community support
- **Pro Tier**: Full features, higher limits, email support
- **Enterprise Tier**: Custom features, unlimited usage, dedicated support, SLA
